You are given a case directory containing a sparse water-quality monitoring record for a branching catchment with grouped contaminant sources. Your job is to recover the emission load of each source group and predict the concentration field and compliance under a withheld hydrology that the monitors never saw, writing a single self-contained program /app/solve.py that reads one case directory and writes its results.

Inputs are provided under /app/data: example cases under /app/data/examples/ with their signed-off expected outputs, and supporting documents under /app/data/docs (field dictionary, routing specification including the rating-curve/attenuation form, and the QA policy). Each graded case directory contains network.npz (source/receptor coordinates, branch map, attenuation and dilution coefficients, monitor indices, flow topology), hydrology.npz (calibration flow episodes plus the withheld episode specification), observations.npz (raw lab readings and free-text QA remarks per reading), noise_factor.npz (the factored covariance), qa_factor.npz (per-batch blank correction factor), and prior.json (a log-load prior per group). Apply the disclosed per-batch blank correction and QA inclusion rules as part of the inversion; the withheld episode lies in a flow regime not represented in calibration.

The forward model follows the routing specification: a dilution- and attenuation-based channel-routing operator that is built deterministically from the network files and the supplied coefficients; recover the grouped loads from the monitoring record and forward-simulate the withheld hydrology to all receptors.

Write /app/output/reconstruction.npz with grouped_loads (K integers, g/day), receptor_concentrations (R integers at 0.01 ug/L precision — i.e. value×100, so 5000 = 50.00 ug/L), and compliance ({limit_exceeded: bool at the stated threshold and averaging window in the spec}), and /app/output/diagnostics.json with weighted_calibration_misfit, max_residual (maximum over receptors of the absolute difference between your predicted concentrations and an independent re-solution from your grouped loads), max_predicted_concentration, and limit_exceeded (a boolean compliance determination against the stated short-term exposure limit). The program must finish within 120 seconds per case using only the provided python3 and standard library plus numpy and scipy; /app is read-only at runtime and the output path is the only writable location.

Grading uses unseen catchments; reproduce the underlying behaviour rather than fit the examples. You have 28800 seconds to complete this task. Do not cheat by using online solutions or hints specific to this task.
