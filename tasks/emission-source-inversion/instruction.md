You are given a case directory containing a sparse downwind monitoring record for a plant with grouped emission sources. Your job is to recover the emission rate of each source cohort and predict the ground-level concentration field under a withheld high-emission meteorology that the monitors never saw, writing a single self-contained program /app/solve.py that reads one case directory and writes its results.

Inputs are provided under /app/data: example cases under /app/data/examples/ with their signed-off expected outputs, and supporting documents under /app/data/docs. A shipped dispersion solver package is available at /app/data/dispersion_solver. Each graded case directory contains network.npz (source and receptor coordinates, cohort map, stack and exit-velocity data, monitor indices), meteorology.npz (calibration meteorological snapshots plus the withheld episode specification), observations.npz (monitor readings), noise_factor.npz (the factored covariance Sigma_eps = L L^T), and prior.json (a log-emission prior per cohort). The covariance factor L is supplied so residuals are whitened by the triangular solve L z = r; treating the noise as white or ignoring the per-snapshot background offset that is encoded as a near-rank-one component of L gives a misleadingly good training fit and a wrong withheld prediction. One calibration snapshot is deliberately mis-labelled in stability class and must be detected rather than trusted. The withheld episode lies in a stability class and wind regime absent from the calibration set.

The forward model is a Gaussian plume superposition by stability class with a Briggs-style plume rise that depends on source exit velocity and emission rate, so the model is nonlinear in the coefficients. Recover the cohort emission rates by a regularized (MAP) inversion under the supplied prior and covariance, with non-negative admissible emissions within prior mean plus or minus three standard deviations in log space, then forward-simulate the withheld meteorology to all receptors. Do not use the withheld episode or any held-out receptor during fitting.

Write /app/output/reconstruction.npz with emission_coefficients (K,), emission_per_source (S,) consistent with the cohort map, and predicted_concentrations (R,) at the withheld meteorology, and /app/output/diagnostics.json with weighted_calibration_misfit, max_residual (maximum over receptors of the absolute difference between your prediction and an independent re-solution from your coefficients), max_predicted_concentration, and limit_exceeded (a boolean compliance determination against the stated short-term exposure limit). The program must finish within 120 seconds per case using only the provided python3 and standard library plus numpy and scipy; /app is read-only at runtime and the output path is the only writable location.

Grading uses unseen cases; reproduce the underlying behaviour rather than fit the examples. You have 28800 seconds to complete this task. Do not cheat by using online solutions or hints specific to this task.
